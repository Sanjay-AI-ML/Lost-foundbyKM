About Our Community Platform

Bridging the gap between honest finders and anxious owners through structured, secure verification.

Our Mission

Every day, thousands of essential belongings—smartphones, keys, wallets, laptops, pets, and cherished documents—are accidentally misplaced. Our mission is to provide an accessible, trustworthy digital lost-and-found hub that simplifies recovery and fosters neighborhood integrity.

How Claims & Verification Work

1. Finding & Reporting

A finder posts the found object with a general description and photo, leaving out sensitive proof markers (like serial numbers or internal contents).

2. Ownership Claim Submission

The rightful owner submits a confidential claim providing proof of ownership (e.g., photo of receipt, IMEI number, exact contents description).

3. Finder Review & Safe Return

The finder reviews submitted claims and approves the true owner. Contact details and rendezvous instructions are safely exchanged.

Safety Recommendations

  • Public Meeting Places: Always meet in broad daylight at public locations (e.g. coffee shops, police station safe zones, campus security desks).
  • Keep Confidential Data Safe: Never publish passwords, full national ID numbers, or bank card security codes in public listings.
  • Verify Before Handover: Check claimant IDs and verify ownership answers before handing over valuable goods.
